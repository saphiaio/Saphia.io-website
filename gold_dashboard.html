The record print is reported as $5,602.22 on 28 January 2026 by one dealer source and $5,597.23 on 29 January by another; the difference is intraday versus close and is immaterial to the analysis.7 The metal then retraced through 2026 as rate expectations shifted; the LBMA average for Q2 2026 was $4,506, still 37% above the same quarter of 2025.4

The short-run driver is monetary: non-yielding bullion is mechanically penalised when real yields rise and rewarded when they fall. The structural driver is the official sector (Section III), which has continued to buy through both halves of the cycle. Reading the two apart is the core analytical task for this market.

The recycling anomaly

Scrap normally responds to price. In 2025 it rose only 3% against a 44% price increase because behaviour diverged by region: developed markets (US, Europe, Japan) sold, while India, the Middle East and South-East Asia held back; among emerging markets only China increased recycling.1 Total above-ground stock is estimated at 219,891 t, the true reserve, since gold is not consumed.13

Sourcing integrity, the MLRO view

The structural loophole is origin attribution: gold is treated as originating where it enters the international market, not where it was mined.16 Dubai refinery capacities of 50–350 t per plant give ample throughput for artisanal doré from Sudan, the Sahel and the Great Lakes, which is why the 2023 due-diligence rules and the 2025 suspensions matter reputationally.16 DMCC's responsible-sourcing rules align with OECD guidance and require enhanced declarations for conflict-affected and high-risk areas such as Mali.17 Med

Analytical parallel: the Kimberley Process has the same origin-laundering weakness, certification attaches to the parcel at first export, not to the mine. The gold trade's answer (LBMA Good Delivery plus refinery-level audits) is closer to what the diamond industry is attempting with source-verification programmes. Both regimes are only as good as the audit of the first-mile aggregator. Hyp
