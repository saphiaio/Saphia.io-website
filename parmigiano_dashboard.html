Why Σ4

A whole wheel is a standardised, consortium-certified, individually numbered unit with a published price by age, and one Italian bank has lent against it for seventy years without a loss. That is more financial infrastructure than most markets on this site. The grade is still Σ4 because the holder must be in the trade: the wheel has to be kept turning in a climate-controlled warehouse, sold into a wholesale market that a private holder cannot reach, and eaten within a few years. There is no route for anyone else.

A food that behaves like a commodity with a bond attached. Every wheel is made to the same recipe in the same five provinces, inspected at twelve months, fire-branded by a consortium, and logged by number; its value rises on a known curve as it ages; and since 1953 Credito Emiliano has taken young wheels as loan collateral, warehoused them, and sold them if the dairy defaults. This page covers the consortium system, the market's size and the 2025 price jump, how the cheese bank works, the fraud and climate risks, and what to watch. No prices are carried as live values.

IThe consortium and the wheel

A PDO that functions as a quality grader, a production regulator and a brand owner at once.

Parmigiano Reggiano is a protected designation of origin: raw cow's milk, salt and calf rennet, from cows fed on local forage, made in about 300 dairies in five provinces of Emilia-Romagna and Lombardy. The Consorzio del Formaggio Parmigiano Reggiano, founded 1934, writes the rules, inspects every wheel at twelve months by tapping it with a hammer, fire-brands those that pass and marks those that do not, operates production-planning quotas on the dairies, and defends the name in court worldwide.56 High

Each wheel carries the dairy number, month and year of production and a casein plate with a unique alphanumeric code, pressed into the rind at making. The wheel is therefore traceable to the day and the vat, which is what makes it bankable. High6

IISize and the 2025–26 cycle

Production up, Italian consumption down, exports past half, prices up a quarter.

The consortium reports 4.19 m wheels in 2025, up 2.7% on 4.079 m; the industry is valued at around $4.7 bn at consumer level. For the first time exports exceeded half of sales, at 50.5% or 74,980 tonnes, with non-EU markets up 4.2%. At home, volumes sold fell 10% as prices rose: Italians bought less often and in smaller pieces, though the number of households buying held. Prices at origin rose 20.6% for 12-month and 24.8% for 24-month wheels.123 High

The cycle is a cattle cycle with a two-year lag. A price rise brings more milk into the PDO, more wheels are made, and two years later the matured supply arrives whether or not demand has held; the consortium's production planning exists to damp this and has not always succeeded (the 2014–15 and 2019–20 price troughs). A holder of wheels is long the 2027 market, not the 2025 one. Hyp

IIIThe cheese bank

Credito Emiliano has lent against wheels since 1953 and says it has never lost a euro.

A dairy pays its farmers every thirty days and sells its cheese a year or more later. Credem bridges the gap: a dairy delivers young wheels to Magazzini Generali delle Tagliate, the bank's warehouse subsidiary at Reggio Emilia and Modena, receives 60–80% of the wheel's value as a loan, and the warehouse turns, brushes and monitors the wheels through maturation, charging a fee. If the dairy defaults, the bank sells the matured cheese. The warehouses hold about 500,000 wheels, worth over €300 m, out of roughly 4 m made a year, and handle 2.3 m wheels a year including non-collateral storage. Since 2024 a blockchain register has allowed wheels to be pledged while remaining in the dairy's own store, which the bank says has doubled its lending capacity.347 High

Parallel to whisky casks and wine in bond: an appreciating, standardised physical asset held by a specialist custodian, with a lender's charge over it. The difference is who the borrower is. In Scotland and Bordeaux the holder of the cask or the case is the investor; in Emilia the holder of the wheel is a dairy with a cash-flow gap, and the bank is the only outside party with a seat.
