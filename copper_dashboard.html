The energy-transition bellwether after a year in which the world's second-largest mine flooded, the forecast surplus became a deficit and then a surplus again, the metal set a record, and the United States put a 50% tariff on semi-finished copper while leaving cathode in limbo. This page covers the balance, the disruption ledger, supply and refining concentration, the corporate consolidation and the policy layer. No prices are carried as live values.

The paper surplus and the record price coexist because stock is maldistributed, not abundant. Macquarie counts visible stocks up 300 kt in 2026 worldwide, all of it the US build; everywhere else they are down 168 kt.31 Med The 2026 benchmark treatment charge settled at $0/t, from $21.25 in 2025, with spot concentrate TCs near −$127/t at end-June: smelters are paying miners for feed, which is why refined output growth is 0.4% while mine output grows 1.6%.30 20 Med

A 100–370 kt swing either way is under 1.3% of a c.29 Mt refined market, but with inelastic demand the marginal tonne sets the price for the whole market, which is why the metal set records in 2026 while Chinese demand was described as muted.5 10 Med Chinese smelters, short of concentrate, cut production; one analysis puts the refined deficit above 350 kt if a 20% Chinese cut persists through Q4 2026.3 Low

The tariff protects a refining base that is shrinking: domestic refined output fell an estimated 9–10% in 2025 on maintenance and grades, and recycled copper covers roughly 30–35% of US supply.13 The undecided cathode tariff has done the real work: the threat alone pulled 1.6 Mt into the US, where it sits stranded, while the rest of the world runs on about 300 kt of exchange stock. Med

Correction, 19 Oct 2026. The launch text described the 50% Section 232 tariff as applying to "cathode, refined copper, alloys and defined downstream products". Refined cathode has been exempt since the August 2025 proclamation; the 50% rate covers semi-finished and derivative products only. The row is restated and the cathode decision is tracked separately.28 29
