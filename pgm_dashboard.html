Platinum Group Metals

Six metals mined together from two geological provinces, priced apart, and consumed mostly inside exhaust systems. Platinum has run three deepening deficits and a fourth is forecast; palladium is heading for surplus; South Africa's largest producer has been spun out of Anglo American. This page covers the balance, supply concentration, demand by end-use, the corporate map and the assumptions behind the outlook. No prices are carried as live values.

The market read was mixed: cleaner exposure for PGM-focused funds, but a producer without a diversified balance sheet to fund the next capital cycle; separation costs for standalone systems were still flowing through in 2025.12 9 Med

Diamond parallel: the same Anglo American restructuring that spun out Valterra put De Beers up for sale. Both are single-region, deep-level, labour-intensive South African businesses whose economics were carried for decades inside a diversified parent; both now face the capital markets alone in the same eighteen months.
