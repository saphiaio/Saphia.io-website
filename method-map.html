The method on one page

Every market is read the same way. Seven questions; four of them scored; six factors weighed into one grade; the result carried onto every brief and tool.

Full methodology → ·

1

Seven questions

Asked of every market, in this order. The dark four are scored.

1SupplyWhat exists, who has it
2DemandWho buys, for what
3ConcentrationWho sets the price
4LiquidityCan a unit be sold
5ScarcityCan supply respond
6ProvenanceIs it what it says
7Price formationHow it all combines
↓
2

Four scores

Each 1 to 5 with a band word. Never added together.

Concentration
1 Low5 High
5 when the top three control three-quarters of the price-setting stage
Liquidity
1 Low5 High
5 when an exchange or daily reference price exists and clears daily
Scarcity
1 Low5 High
5 when supply is fixed or falling and cannot expand in five years
Provenance risk
1 Low5 High
5 when mislabelling exceeds a fifth, or no independent check exists
