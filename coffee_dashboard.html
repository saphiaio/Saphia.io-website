Coffee

The world's most traded soft commodity after a two-year spike that took arabica to an all-time record in October 2025, followed by a 38% fall and two rallies inside 2026. Record production, record consumption, five consecutive years of falling stocks, a 50% US tariff on Brazilian beans, and the consolidation of the roasting industry into two global champions. IIIDemand and trade structure

A concentrated buy-side facing millions of smallholders.

Fourteen companies, Caravela, ECOM, Export Trading Company, illycaffè, JDE Peet's, Louis Dreyfus, Neumann Kaffee Gruppe, ofi, Sucafina, Sucden Coffee, Taylors of Harrogate, Touton, UCC and Volcafe, described by one trade commentator as "the entire concentrated buy-side of the global green coffee market", signed a joint procurement framework in April 2026 with no minimum price or enforcement mechanism.8 Med, single opinionated source; the list of signatories is factual, the characterisation is the author's.
