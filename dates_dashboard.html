Dates

A ten-million-tonne crop grown almost entirely in the Middle East and North Africa, of which the world's largest producer exports almost nothing and the second-largest has turned exports into a Vision 2030 target. This page covers production and its concentration, the export trade, the variety premium that separates a religious commodity from a supermarket one, the Gulf's producing and re-exporting role, and the integrity questions of a crop sold largely by name. No prices are carried as live values.

The premium is not about sugar or size. Ajwa carries a religious association that makes demand insensitive to price; Medjool carries a Western retail brand built in California and Israel; Deglet Nour carries a European habit. Everything below those tiers competes on cost, which is why Egypt, with the largest crop, has the weakest export position.9 3 Med

Diamond parallel: "Ajwa from Medina" is a provenance claim that multiplies the value of a gradable agricultural product, in the same way "Kashmir" does for a sapphire, and, like a sapphire, the claim is made on a label the buyer cannot verify at the point of sale. The Saudi state's answer is the same as Botswana's: put the brand under public ownership and control the supply chain from tree to tin. Hyp

VIntegrity

A crop sold by variety name and origin, with no grading authority outside the exporting state.

The premium tiers are defined by a place-name (Medina, Qassim, Biskra) and a cultivar name that a consumer cannot test. Saudi exporters supply phytosanitary certificates from the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture together with ISO 22000, HACCP and halal certification for container trade, which certify safety and process rather than variety or origin.14 No independent audit of variety mislabelling in the international date trade was found for this page, and none is asserted. Med

Compliance reading: for a Gulf re-exporter the exposure is origin laundering, bulk Egyptian or Iranian fruit repacked under a Gulf label, which customs data would show as the historical gap between gross and net UAE exports. The control is the same as for gold doré: match declared origin to the import manifest, not to the retail box. Hyp
