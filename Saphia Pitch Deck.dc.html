The method

Every sector opens on the same Market Snapshot.

Eight fields, four of them rated. Each rating carries a confidence tag and cites the section that evidences it.

Four scales, rated Low / Medium / High.

Scale A
Supply concentration
Where the power to set price sits.
Scale B
Liquidity
Whether a unit converts to cash at a knowable price.
Scale C
Scarcity
Whether supply can respond to price, and how fast.
Scale D
Provenance risk
The chance origin or grade is misstated.

"No composite score. A composite would require weighting four scales against each other, and no single weighting survives every market."
