Conflicts and independence

One page on who pays for what, and on what cannot be bought.

Who paysSaphia Intelligence funds the research. It sells access to data and tools to private banks, custodians, advisers and wealth-reporting platforms. No market, producer, dealer, auction house, fund or platform pays for coverage, and none is offered coverage.
AdvertisingNone. No sponsored content, affiliate links or referral fees, in any form, on any page.
Commissioned workA client may commission research on a market or a holding. The output is labelled "Commissioned" with the client type (not the client) and the date. A commission cannot start, stop, delay or move a published grade or scale score, and the register of grades is never gated.
What moves a gradeOnly the method. A grade or a scale score changes when the evidence recorded on the brief changes, when the method version changes, or when a correction is logged. Every change is entered in the ratings history with its rationale and method version.
HoldingsSaphia Intelligence does not hold, trade or advise on any asset in the register, and does not take positions on the basis of unpublished work.
Right of replyA producer body, exchange, house or platform named in a brief may reply. Replies are published, dated, at the foot of the brief, separated from ΣΟΦΙΑ's text and unedited.
