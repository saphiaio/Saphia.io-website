Tea

The world's most consumed manufactured drink after water: seven million tonnes a year, more than half of it Chinese, priced at commodity auctions in Mombasa and Kolkata, and, at the top, a Japanese shade-grown powder whose producer price rose 2.6-fold in a single year. This page covers the overview: production and its concentration, the auction system, the export trade, and the matcha shortage as the clearest case of a luxury tier decoupling from its commodity base. The auction floor

Black tea is priced at producer-country auctions, Mombasa (the world's largest, over 500 kt a year), Kolkata, Guwahati, Colombo, where brokers sell graded lots to blenders and packers. In 2024 the International Tea Committee reported Mombasa heavily glutted with unsold, "unfresh" teas passing auction after auction, while African import markets (Egypt −16%, Morocco −27%) contracted; producing countries have responded by promoting domestic consumption to rebalance supply and demand.5 High The FAO expects Kenya to keep its export leadership over the decade and India to overtake Sri Lanka as the second black-tea exporter.3

Diamond parallel: the Mombasa auction is a rough tender for a perishable, graded lots, sealed bids, a handful of large buyers, and a producer base of smallholders who take the price they are given. The unsold-lot problem of 2024 is the same signal as unsold sight boxes: the intermediary has stopped absorbing inventory. Hyp

2026
First-flush auctions confirm the squeeze: Kyoto tencha from ¥8,235 to c.¥13,972/kg, national aggregate c.¥14,541, Kagoshima c.¥19,000; a Shizuoka top lot at ¥1.18 m/kg; Kagoshima's opening auction the highest since 1989. Uji reports one of its strongest harvests in decades, and prices do not fall.8 13

The supply response is a conversion, not a planting: Shizuoka's Ashikubo cooperative switched a sencha line to tencha in late 2025 for ¥200 m after tencha sold for c.¥3,800/kg against c.¥300 for its sencha, more than ten times.8 Ippodo expects more capacity to shift to matcha in 2026, which will tighten sencha and bancha in turn.7 Kagoshima has become the B2B standard for high-quality, cost-efficient matcha, while Uji remains the prestige tier; direct exporters undercut traditional distributors materially.11 Med
