Wagyu

A farmed animal graded so rigorously that the top grade has stopped being scarce: three-quarters of the Japanese market is now A5, and the growth is in export. This page covers the breed registry, the grading regime, the brand councils that sit above it, and the export trade. No prices are carried as live values.

Every retail cut carries a seal and a ten-digit traceability number that can be checked on a phone, a provenance system the diamond trade is still building.2 Regional cooperatives control breeding, feed and marketing, which sustains the brand mystique but makes quality metrics hard to standardise internationally.9 High

Japan's export strategy set a target of ¥2 trillion in agricultural exports by 2025 and ¥5 trillion by 2030, with wagyu among the flagship products.2 At home, A5 saturates the market as inflation-hit consumers shy away from the most expensive cuts, which is why producers are pushing the top grade into export; pandemic-era inventories have returned to near pre-pandemic levels.8 1 Restaurant wagyu can price at four to ten times USDA Prime, and A4 is the volume workhorse of export at 35–50% below A5; crossbred F1 wagyu trades at a discount regardless of marbling.9 5 Med

IIIBrand economics compared

What the two markets share, and where they diverge.

DimensionBluefin tunaWagyu
Supply controlInternational quotas (ICCAT, WCPFC, CCSBT); wild stock plus ranchingBreed registry and prefectural cooperatives; farmed
GradingBuyer inspection at auction, colour, texture, fat, tail cutJMGA carcass grade; BMS 1–12; brand councils
Price discoveryDaily Toyosu auction; ceremonial New Year bidWholesale carcass auctions; brand premiums on top
Provenance markerLanding port (Ōma) and methodPrefecture, strain (Tajima), ten-digit serial
SubstituteFarmed / ranched bluefin; other tunasAustralian and American wagyu; F1 crossbreds
Scarcity signalRising ceremonial price, falling commercial priceA5 at 75%+ of market: the top grade has stopped being scarce

In both cases the Japanese system grades rigorously and then lets a place-name or a ceremony carry the luxury premium above the grade. The bluefin premium is defensible because the wild fish is genuinely finite; the wagyu premium is under pressure because the grade it rests on has become the norm.8 3 Hyp
