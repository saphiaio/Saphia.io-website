About

ΣΟΦΙΑ publishes cited, confidence-rated intelligence on a wide variety of scarce-asset markets, diamonds, gold, copper, rare earths, fine wine, Scotch whisky, saffron, oud, caviar and others that share almost nothing except how they should be read.

The point isn't how many markets we cover. It's that we look at all of them the same way: supply, demand, concentration, liquidity, scarcity, provenance, price formation, the same seven questions in the same order, no matter the market.

How

Every quantitative claim is cited. Every rating carries a confidence tag and a link to the evidence behind it. Where sources disagree, both figures are shown and the choice is explained rather than reconciled away. The full method is published, not summarised.
