Privacy

Effective 2 Nov 2026

The short version: this site does not track you.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The site is static: no cookies, no analytics, no accounts, no forms. The report builder runs in your browser and sends us nothing.

Two things happen in the background. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it serves them. And our host, [HOST], keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for a short period for security. We do not look at these beyond that.

If what we collect ever changes, this page and its date will too.
